About HeicGo

HeicGo makes small, sharp image tools that run entirely in your browser. Our first tool — a HEIC to JPG converter — was born from a simple observation: hundreds of millions of iPhone photos are trapped in a format that Windows and the web still struggle to open, and most "free" online converters make you upload private photos to a server just to change a file format.

We think that's backwards. Converting a photo doesn't require a server. So HeicGo does it on your device, using WebAssembly — your photos never leave your browser, and the tool even works offline once loaded.

Our principles

  • Privacy is the default. Files are processed locally; there is no upload step to remove, because there never was one.
  • Free means free. No signup, no watermark, no daily limits, no "premium" unlock.
  • Fast beats fancy. One page, one job, done in seconds.

What's next

HeicGo currently serves 8 languages (English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese) and we're steadily adding tools. If an image task annoys you, chances are we're working on it.
